About JHP Construction

Field Experience.
Professional Results.

JHP Construction LLC is an Oklahoma-based fiber optic construction and telecommunications contractor serving telecoms, electric cooperatives, internet service providers, prime contractors, and network operators across Oklahoma and surrounding markets.

We bring hands-on field experience to every project — from individual service locations to large-scale fiber deployments. Our crews are trained, equipped, and ready to deliver accurate documentation, clean workmanship, and responsive communication from first contact to final test.

When you call JHP, you get a contractor who shows up, does the work right, and stands behind it.

What We Do

Our Services

Full-spectrum fiber optic construction and splicing services — from individual drops to large-scale OSP deployments.

Fiber Optic Fusion Splicing

Precision single-fiber fusion splicing with full loss documentation.

Ribbon & Mass Fusion Splicing

High-count ribbon fiber splicing for large-scale deployments.

Fiber Splice Case Building & Re-Entry

New builds and re-entry on existing splice enclosures.

CommScope 450D Case Builds

Experienced builds on CommScope 450D dome closures.

FTTH Residential & Commercial Premise Installation

End-to-end fiber-to-the-home and commercial premise drops.

Aerial Fiber Drop Installation

Lashed and self-supporting aerial drop installations.

Underground Fiber Drop Installation

Directional bore and open-cut underground fiber drops.

Existing Conduit Fiber Pulls

Fiber placement through existing conduit infrastructure.

OTDR Testing & Documentation

Full OTDR trace documentation for every splice and segment.

Power Meter & Light Source Testing

End-to-end optical loss testing with calibrated equipment.

Fiber Troubleshooting

Fault isolation and resolution on live and dark fiber.

Emergency Fiber Restoration

Rapid response for outages — available when it matters most.
